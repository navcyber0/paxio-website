Paxio Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Paxio ("we," "us," "the app") is a parental control app that helps a parent manage a child's Android device — screen time, app blocking, and content filtering. This policy explains what data Paxio collects, why, and where it goes. Paxio is installed and configured by a parent or guardian; it does not create a separate account for the child.

What we don't collect

Paxio does not collect location data, does not read screen content, keystrokes, messages, photos, or full browsing history (only whether a specific site visit was blocked, and its category), and does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

AI-generated insights (not currently active)

Paxio has an AI Insights feature planned for a future paid tier — it isn't available to any user today, so no automated analysis of usage data currently takes place. If and when this feature launches, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what it processes and where, before it's enabled for any account.

Data sharing

We don't sell data or share it with advertisers. Data above is processed by Google/Firebase solely to provide sign-in, profile sync, and crash reporting, under Google's own privacy terms.

Data retention & deletion

Child profile and PIN data are kept until the parent deletes the child profile or account. Usage history, blocked-site visits, and blocked-app attempts are automatically deleted after 30 days, both locally and in Firestore. Local, on-device data is also removed when Paxio is uninstalled. A parent can delete a single child's data, or their whole account, at any time directly in the app — see Delete Your Data and Delete Your Account for exactly what's removed.

Children's privacy

Paxio is installed and configured by a parent or guardian. The child does not create an account, sign in, or provide information directly to Paxio.

Permissions Paxio requests

Accessibility Service, Usage Access, Device Admin, and VPN are requested with an in-app explanation before use, for app blocking, screen-time tracking, uninstall protection, and content filtering respectively.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@paxio.in.
