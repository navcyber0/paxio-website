Screen Time Limit

A daily screen time limit that actually enforces itself

Set a time limit for your kid's screen time once, in 15-minute steps from 30 minutes to 24 hours, and Paxio enforces it automatically — no reminders, no manual check-ins.

One number, enforced automatically

Paxio is the app that controls screen time on your kid's Android phone from one screen. Set a time limit for their screen time once, in 15-minute steps from 30 minutes to 24 hours, and it enforces itself — no reminders, no manual check-ins.

The details

How it actually works

What counts against the limit

Every app on the device counts toward the daily total, with one deliberate exception.

  • Phone calls and text messages never count — your kid can always reach you.
  • The limit resets automatically at midnight, no manual reset needed.

What your kid sees

The limit isn't a secret — it's visible and predictable.

  • A countdown ring on the child dashboard shows exactly how much time is left today.
  • Your child can request more time right from the lock screen — no need to find you in person.
  • That request lands on your phone instantly for a one-tap approval or denial, from anywhere, fully online — nothing needs you physically nearby, and the change applies automatically the moment you approve it.

FAQ

Screen Time Limit — common questions

Does the screen time limit apply to every app?

Yes, with one exception: phone calls and texts never count against the daily limit.

What happens when the limit is reached?

The device locks to a simple home screen showing the limit was reached, with calls and texts still available. Your child can send an extra-time request right from that screen — it lands on your phone for a one-tap approval or denial, from anywhere.

Can I set a different limit for weekends?

Not as a separate weekend schedule today — but the streak bonus adds extra minutes automatically as your child stays under the daily limit for a run of days.

Can my child see how much time they have left?

Yes — the child dashboard shows a live countdown ring, so there are no surprises when the limit is close.
