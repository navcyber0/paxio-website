The details

How it actually works

Three time ranges

Zoom in on today, or step back for the trend.

  • Daily, Weekly, and Monthly views, each with its own trend chart.
  • Free shows 3 stats and 30 days of history; Pro unlocks all seven stats and a full year.
  • Switching ranges doesn't require leaving the Reports screen — it's a tab switch.

The seven stat tiles

Beyond just “screen time.”

  • Total screen time, over-limit days, and blocked-app attempts.
  • Days inside the limit, latest phone use, and average session length.
  • Extra-time requests sent — a signal for whether the current limit fits your child's routine.

The weekly email digest

The same numbers, without opening the app.

  • A weekly recap lands in your inbox automatically, roughly once a week.
  • It's opt-in per event type from Settings → Email Notifications, so it's easy to turn off or narrow down.
  • This is the same underlying data as the Reports screen, not a separate summary.

FAQ

Reports Screen — common questions

What's the difference between Free and Pro reports?

Free shows 3 stats and 30 days of history. Pro unlocks all seven stat tiles and a full year of history.

Do I need to open the app to see my child's weekly report?

No — a weekly digest with the same numbers is emailed automatically, and you can opt in or out per event type from Settings.

What counts as a blocked-app attempt on the Reports screen?

Any time your child opens an app that's set to Blocked, it's logged as an attempt and shown on the Reports screen.

Can I see reports for more than one child?

Yes — each child profile has its own separate Reports screen, since each has its own limits and usage.
