Looking for an AirDroid Parental Control alternative?

Last updated September 2026

AirDroid Parental Control is one of the most feature-maximalist options in the category: remote camera access, screen mirroring, live audio, real-time GPS location with geofencing, and calls/SMS monitoring, all in one app — plus, on Android, an official stealth mode that hides the app from the child's home screen entirely. That's a genuinely different product philosophy from Paxio, which is built to stay visible to the child and deliberately leaves out remote camera, audio, and location tracking. If AirDroid's scope feels like more than you actually want, here's the honest comparison.

Quick answer

Pick Paxio if: you want a parental control app your kid knows is installed — screen time limits, app blocking, content filtering, and bedtime scheduling — without remote camera/audio access, live location tracking, or a hidden app icon.

Pick AirDroid if: you specifically want real-time device supervision — remote screen/camera access, live location and route history, and message monitoring — and you're comfortable with (or specifically want) a stealth-mode option that runs hidden on your child's Android device.

Where AirDroid wins

  • Real-time device supervision. Remote camera, screen mirroring, and live audio go far beyond what Paxio does or aims to do.
  • Location tracking. Live GPS location, geofence alerts, and route history are core, advertised features.
  • Message and chat monitoring. Calls, SMS, and AI-flagged chat content are covered, which Paxio doesn't include on any tier.
  • Cross-platform. Manages both Android and iOS devices, with feature availability varying by platform.
  • Track record. 10M+ installs on Android alone and a mature, wide feature set.

Where Paxio wins

  • Nothing hidden. Paxio is installed openly and stays visible on the child's device — there's no stealth-mode option, by design. See what a parental control app actually is for why that distinction matters.
  • No remote camera, audio, or screen access. If AirDroid's real-time supervision feels like more visibility than you want, Paxio simply doesn't have those capabilities to turn on.
  • No location tracking, ever. Not a togglable extra — it isn't collected.
  • On-device content filtering. DNS-level filtering happens on the device itself — general browsing never leaves it, only the fact that something was blocked, and its category.
  • A published price, no trial gate. ₹499/mo or ₹4,999/yr, visible before you install anything — and the free tier is a real, usable set of controls, not a 3-day countdown.
  • Weekly report by email, not just in-app numbers.

Common questions

Is Paxio a good AirDroid Parental Control alternative? If your needs stop at screen time, app blocking, content filtering, and bedtime scheduling on Android — without remote camera, live location, or a hidden app — yes, with a published price. If you specifically want AirDroid's remote-supervision or location features, Paxio isn't a full replacement.

Does AirDroid Parental Control hide itself from the child's phone? Yes — AirDroid's own site advertises a stealth mode for Android that runs the app hidden from the home screen. Paxio does the opposite by design.

Does AirDroid have remote camera and location tracking? Yes, both are advertised, core features. Paxio doesn't include either, on any tier.

Paxio is free to try for one child — screen time, app blocking, and content filtering, running entirely on the device, visible the whole time.
