Paxio vs Qustodio: a Qustodio alternative worth considering?

Last updated September 2026

If you're searching for a Qustodio alternative, it's usually for one of two reasons: the price, or the amount of data Qustodio collects (location, calls, texts) for something you just wanted to manage screen time on. Qustodio itself is one of the oldest, most feature-complete parental control apps around — cross-platform, location tracking, call/text monitoring, deep reporting. Paxio is newer and deliberately narrower: Android-only, on-device content filtering, no location tracking, no message reading. Neither is "better" in the abstract — they're built for different amounts of visibility. Here's the honest Paxio vs Qustodio breakdown.

Quick answer

Pick Paxio if: you're Android-only, want screen time + app blocking + content filtering + bedtime done well, and don't want location tracking or message monitoring as part of the deal.

Pick Qustodio if: your family is mixed iOS/Android, you want one dashboard for both, or you specifically need location tracking or call/text visibility alongside device controls.

Where Qustodio wins

  • Cross-platform. One dashboard covering Android, iOS, and desktop — Paxio is Android-only, full stop.
  • Breadth. Location tracking and call/text monitoring on top of device controls, if you want one app that does nearly everything.
  • Maturity. Qustodio has been in this category for years, with the reporting depth and blocklist tuning that comes with that.

Where Paxio wins

  • Price. Paxio Pro (₹499/mo or ₹4,999/yr) is generally cheaper than Qustodio's comparable paid tier.
  • Privacy scope. No location tracking, no message reading — if that breadth is something you'd rather not have on your kid's phone at all, Paxio doesn't collect it in the first place, not as an opt-out.
  • On-device content filtering. DNS-level filtering happens on the device itself — general browsing never leaves it, only the fact that something was blocked, and its category.
  • Genuinely free tier. Real, usable controls for one child with no card required, not a 7-day trial dressed up as free.
  • Streak bonus. A kid who stays under their daily limit for a run of days earns bonus screen time automatically — a small incentive layer Qustodio doesn't have an equivalent of.
  • Weekly report by email, not just a dashboard you have to remember to check.
  • A real child-facing dashboard. Your kid sees their own time-left ring, streak, and where their time went — not a parent-only monitoring view with nothing surfaced back to them.

Common questions

Is Paxio a good Qustodio alternative? If your needs stop at screen time, app blocking, content filtering, and bedtime scheduling on Android, yes — at a lower price. If you need cross-platform, location, or message monitoring, Paxio isn't a full replacement.

Does Qustodio have a free plan? A limited one — most content-filtering categories and full reporting sit behind its paid plans. Paxio's free tier includes a real daily limit, blocking up to 5 apps, and 3 content categories, no trial clock running.

Does Paxio track location like Qustodio? No, on any tier, by design — not an opt-in add-on that's simply turned off.

Paxio is free to try for one child — screen time, app blocking, and content filtering, running entirely on the device.
