Paxio vs Google Family Link: is it worth switching?

Last updated September 2026

Family Link is free, made by Google, and about as frictionless as parental controls get if your family already runs on Android and Google accounts. Most people searching for a Family Link alternative have hit one specific gap: content filtering that only covers Google's own surfaces, or a device-wide time limit when what they actually want is per-app limits. Here's an honest Paxio vs Family Link comparison, including where Family Link is still the right call.

Quick answer

Stick with Family Link if: you want free, zero-setup basics — app approval before install, a device-wide time limit, location — and don't need deeper content filtering or per-app time limits.

Switch to (or add) Paxio if: you want on-device content filtering that works across every app and browser, per-app time limits instead of one global timer, or bedtime scheduling as its own separate control.

Where Family Link wins

  • It's free, with zero upsell. No paid tier to consider, ever.
  • Setup friction is the lowest in the category. If your family is already on Android with Google accounts, it's close to already set up.
  • App install approval. A child can't install a new app at all without parent sign-off — a different, earlier checkpoint than app blocking after the fact.
  • Location tracking, if that's something you want alongside device controls.

Where Paxio wins

Family Link covers bedtime, bonus time, and a remote pause too — the difference is mostly in how targeted and automatic Paxio's versions are:

  • Content filtering depth. On-device DNS-level filtering blocks by category across every app and browser — not limited to what Google SafeSearch and Play Store ratings can catch.
  • Per-app time limits. Instead of one global daily timer, cap specific apps individually (Pro tier).
  • Bedtime as its own control, separate from the daily limit — a recurring schedule or a one-off timer for homework, not the same setting doing two jobs.
  • Remote internet pause that's internet-specific, not a whole-device lock — the phone still works for everything except getting online.
  • No location tracking, if you specifically don't want that as part of the deal.
  • Automatic streak bonus — bonus screen time is granted automatically for staying under budget a run of days, rather than a parent manually approving a bonus-time request each time.
  • Richer child-facing dashboard — Family Link's companion app covers the basics (time left, requesting more), but Paxio's also shows a streak and where the day's time actually went, per app.

Common questions

Is Paxio a good Family Link alternative? If Family Link's content filtering or single device-wide timer isn't enough, yes. If you just need the free basics, Family Link still covers that well.

Does Family Link have content filtering like Paxio? Not at the same depth — it leans on Google SafeSearch and Play Store ratings rather than category-based blocking across every app and browser.

Can Family Link set per-app time limits? Only in a limited way — Paxio's Pro tier is built specifically for per-app limits.

A lot of families actually run both — Family Link for install approval and location, Paxio for the content filtering and per-app limits Family Link doesn't cover as deeply. They're not mutually exclusive.
