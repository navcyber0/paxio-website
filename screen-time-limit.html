The details

How it actually works

What counts against the limit

Every app on the device counts toward the daily total, with two deliberate exceptions.

  • Phone calls and text messages never count — your kid can always reach you.
  • Anything in the school folder (set during onboarding) is excluded, so schoolwork apps don't eat into the budget.
  • The limit resets automatically at midnight, no manual reset needed.

Streak bonus

Good habits earn a real reward, not just a restriction.

  • 5 days in a row under the daily limit earns 20 bonus weekend minutes.
  • The streak is visible to your child on their own dashboard, building anticipation.
  • Bonus minutes never apply themselves — they land in your approval queue first.

What your kid sees

The limit isn't a secret — it's visible and predictable.

  • A countdown ring on the child dashboard shows exactly how much time is left today.
  • No surprise lockouts: the same dimming warning used for bedtime applies as the limit gets close.
  • Extra-time requests go straight to your Overview screen for a one-tap approval or denial.

FAQ

Screen Time Limit — common questions

Does the screen time limit apply to every app?

Yes, with two exceptions: phone calls/texts and anything in the school folder set during onboarding never count against the daily limit.

What happens when the limit is reached?

The device locks to a simple home screen showing the limit was reached, with calls and texts still available. Your child can send an extra-time request from that screen.

Can I set a different limit for weekends?

Not as a separate weekend schedule today — but streak bonuses add extra weekend minutes on top of the daily limit when your child stays under budget during the week.

Can my child see how much time they have left?

Yes — the child dashboard shows a live countdown ring, so there are no surprises when the limit is close.
