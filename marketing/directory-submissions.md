## 1. AlternativeTo

**What it is**: a site where people search "alternative to X" and see competing products —
exactly matches how people search when evaluating a switch from Qustodio/Family Link/Bark.

**How to submit**: go to alternativeto.net, sign up, click "Suggest a new app" (or search "Paxio"
first to confirm it's not already listed by someone else, then "Add it").

**Fields to fill in**:

- **App name**: Paxio
- **Short description** (one-liner, ~100 chars):
  ```
  Android parental control app — screen time limits, app blocking, on-device content filtering, bedtime locks.
  ```
- **Full description**:
  ```
  Paxio is a parental control app for Android that sets screen time limits, blocks distracting
  apps, filters content on-device, and enforces bedtime schedules. Free tier covers the core
  controls for one child profile — no card required. Pro unlocks up to 3 child profiles,
  unlimited app blocking with per-app time limits, full content-filter categories, and remote
  internet pause.

  Built as a lighter, more focused alternative to bigger all-in-one suites — no location
  tracking, no message reading, just the core screen-time and content controls done well.
  ```
- **License/pricing model**: Freemium
- **Platforms**: Android
- **Tags**: parental-control, screen-time, android, family-safety, content-filtering,
  app-blocking, kids-safety
- **"Paxio is an alternative to"** — list these so it surfaces on their comparison pages:
  Qustodio, Google Family Link, Bark, Norton Family, Net Nanny, AirDroid Parental Control,
  FamiSafe
- **Website**: https://www.paxio.in/
- **Source/download link**: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.paxio

## 3. Product Hunt

**What it is**: a real launch, not just a listing — timing and the maker's own first comment
matter a lot here. Best treated as a proper mini-launch day, not a five-minute form fill.

**How to submit**: producthunt.com → create a account → "Submit" a new product. Schedule the
launch (Product Hunt lets you pick a future date — Tuesday-Thursday tends to get the most
traffic, avoid Mon/Fri/weekends).

**Tagline** (max 60 characters):
```
Screen time and content controls for kids, without the bloat
```

**Description** (trimmed to fit Product Hunt's 500-character limit — 495 chars):
```
Paxio is an Android parental control app — screen time limits, app blocking, on-device content filtering, and bedtime scheduling. Free for one child, no card required.

No location tracking, no message reading — just the core controls, done well. Content filtering is on-device DNS-level, working across every app and browser, not just Chrome.

Pro (₹499/mo) adds more profiles, unlimited app blocking, and remote internet pause.

Feedback welcome, especially from Xiaomi/Realme/Oppo/Vivo users!
```

**Topics/tags to select**: Parenting, Android, Family, Productivity, Security

**Maker's first comment** (post this immediately when it goes live — Product Hunt's algorithm and
community both respond to an active maker presence, not just a static listing):
```
Hey everyone 👋 — maker here.

Built Paxio after getting frustrated with parental control apps that either cost more than they
should for the basics, or bundle in location tracking/message reading I never wanted in the
first place.

Free tier covers real screen time limits, app blocking (up to 5 apps), and content filtering
(3 categories) for one child — genuinely usable, not a crippled trial.

Happy to answer anything about how it works under the hood (it's DNS-level on-device filtering +
Accessibility Service for app blocking, no VPN-routing-your-traffic-through-us weirdness) or take
feature requests. What's the #1 thing you wish your current parental control app did better?
```
