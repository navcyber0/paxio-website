Hoverwatch, Canopy, SafeWise, ParentalEdge, Boomerang. Search each site directly for their
existing "best parental control apps" or "Qustodio alternatives" post before emailing — confirm

## Email template

**Subject options** (pick one, keep it specific — generic "collaboration" subject lines get
ignored):

```

[Your name]
```

**Notes**:
- Keep it short — roundup authors get a lot of "please add my product" emails; specificity (naming
  their actual post, one genuine detail from it) is what gets it read past the first line.
- Never ask them to remove or downrank a competitor, never offer payment/reciprocal links (both
  read as manipulative and can get the email ignored or the post flagged) — just ask honestly to
  be considered.
- Track sent emails below so the same site isn't contacted twice or forgotten about.
