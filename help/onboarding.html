Onboarding: setting up your first child profile

Last updated: 26 August 2026

After you sign up, Paxio walks you through a short guided setup before you reach your dashboard: a parent PIN, whose phone this is, your first child's profile, suggested settings, permissions (on a child's device), and a one-time look at Pro.

Create your parent PIN

  1. Enter a 4-digit PIN. This keeps your parental controls private from your child.
  2. Enter it again to confirm — you'll see "Confirm your PIN" on the same screen.

Choose whose phone this is

  1. "This is my phone" — manage your kids' devices remotely from here. Most parents start with this.
  2. "This is my child's phone" — monitoring runs on this device, managed remotely from your own phone.
  3. "We share this phone" — one device for both of you; switch between parent and child view with your PIN.

Add your child's profile

  1. Pick an avatar from the grid.
  2. Enter your child's name and set their age with the slider.
  3. Tap "See suggested settings" — Paxio proposes age-appropriate limits you can adjust before saving.

Review suggested settings

  1. Paxio proposes settings for your child's age — a screen timer, sleep timer, content-filter band, and app blocker starting point.
  2. Tap "Use these settings" to accept them and continue. Every row stays editable later from the Control tab.

Grant permissions (child's device only)

This step only appears on a device set to "This is my child's phone," or a shared device switched into child view — a "This is my phone" device never sees it, since permissions are only needed to run enforcement on a child-facing device.

  1. Paxio explains each permission — Accessibility Service, Usage Access, Device Admin, and Notifications — before asking for it.
  2. Grant each one in Android's own permission dialogs. Monitoring starts once all of them are granted.

Try Paxio Pro

  1. Shown once, right after your first child's settings are set.
  2. Compare Free and Pro — more child profiles, unlimited app blocking, full content filtering, bedtime locks, internet control, and deeper reports.
  3. Tap "Continue to Paxio Pro" to upgrade, or "Skip for now" — you can upgrade later from Settings.

Your dashboard

  1. Setup is done. You land on the Overview tab — your dashboard for that child's screen time, quick controls, and reports.
  2. Add more children, or change any of the above later, from Settings.
