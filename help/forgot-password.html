Resetting a forgotten Paxio password

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This applies if you signed up with email and password. If you signed up with Google, there's no separate Paxio password to reset — use Google's own account recovery instead.

Request a reset link

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" below the password field.
  2. Enter the email address you signed up with in the dialog that appears.
  3. Tap "Send Reset Email."

Finish resetting your password

  1. Look for a confirmation on the sign-in screen: "Password reset email sent — check your inbox."
  2. Open the email Paxio sends you and follow the link to choose a new password.
  3. Return to Paxio and sign in with your new password.
