Settings, section by section

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Settings opens from the icon on Overview or the bottom nav, and covers your account, this device's role, security, and app info — grouped into a few short sections.

Profile & Paxio Pro

The top card shows your name, email, and Free/Pro status (with renewal date, if Pro). Free accounts see a Paxio Pro card right below it — tapping through opens the same pricing screen shown during onboarding.

This Device — changing your device type

  1. Tap "Change Device Type" — its subtitle shows the current role (or "Not set up").
  2. Tap "Continue" on the warning dialog — this stops any active monitoring on this device.
  3. Enter your parent PIN to confirm.
  1. Pick the new role — "This is my phone," "This is my child's phone," or "We share this phone," same as onboarding.
  2. Choosing "This is my child's phone" then asks which child profile this device belongs to.

Security & App

Account

Sign Out and Delete Account sit together near the bottom. Signing out just signs out of your Google account. Deleting your account is permanent — see Deleting your account for the full walkthrough.

About

About Paxio shows the version and a short data-handling summary; Privacy Policy and Terms of Service open the same pages linked in this site's footer. The current version number and device-mode label also sit quietly at the very bottom of the screen.
