Reading the Reports screen

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Reports breaks down usage into Daily, Weekly, and Monthly views, each with a trend chart up top and a grid of stat tiles below it.

Choosing a period

  1. Daily, Weekly, or Monthly tabs switch the whole screen's granularity.
  2. The date pill (e.g. "Aug 26") opens a calendar — for Weekly, picking any day selects that whole week; Monthly only needs a month, not a specific day.
  3. Arrows either side of the date step to the previous or next period.

What each stat means

StatWhat it's measuring
Screen time trendA bar chart of usage across the period — by hour for Daily, by day for Weekly/Monthly.
Screen timeTotal minutes used in the period.
Over-limit daysHow many days usage exceeded the daily limit.
Blocked app attemptsHow many times a blocked app was opened and stopped.
Days inside the limitThe flip side of over-limit days — how many days stayed within the limit.
Latest phone useThe most recent time the device was used.
Avg session lengthAverage continuous stretch of use.
Requests sentHow many "ask for more time" / unblock / bedtime-pause requests your child sent in the period.

A Free plan sees 3 of these stats and 30 days of history; Pro unlocks all 7 stats and a full year — see Pricing for the full comparison.
